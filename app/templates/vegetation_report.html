Understanding NDVI and Land Cover Classification

The NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) is a standardized index that helps us identify and analyze vegetation cover. It ranges from -1 to +1, where higher values indicate healthier and denser vegetation.

NDVI Range Classification Description
-1.0 to 0.0 Water contained areas Water bodies, Irrigated land, or snow.
0.0 to 0.2 Bare soil or sparse vegetation Very minimal vegetation, often arid regions.
0.2 to 0.4 Moderate vegetation Grasslands, shrubs, or sparse crops.
0.4 to 0.6 Healthy vegetation Agricultural areas, forests, or dense shrubs.
0.6 to 1.0 Very dense vegetation Tropical rainforests or areas with extremely lush vegetation.

Quarterly Analysis Definition

The quarterly analysis divides the year into four periods:

  • Q1: January to March
  • Q2: April to June
  • Q3: July to September
  • Q4: October to December
